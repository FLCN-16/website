X · JetBrains Mono everywhere

Headline, body, nav, labels — all in JBM. Strong "engineering document" personality, top-to-bottom. Distinctive, opinionated, less common.

Trade-off: mono body text is ~10% less efficient to read at length (every glyph the same width). Fine on a portfolio where blocks are short. Can feel tiring on Blog posts.

Y · JetBrains Mono for accents, Inter for prose (recommended)

JBM lives in the rail, eyebrows, labels, stats numbers, code, and status pills. Inter handles headlines and body. Engineering personality where it counts, readable prose where it matters.

Trade-off: two fonts means ~50KB of additional font payload, which is negligible (we subset and self-host via next/font).
