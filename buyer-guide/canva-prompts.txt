CODECANYON ASSET PROMPTS — payload-portfolio
=============================================
Use these prompts with Canva via Claude to generate all required
CodeCanyon marketplace images. Run each block as a separate Canva
generate request.

Required sizes:
  Preview image  : 590 × 300 px  (JPEG, shown in search results)
  Cover / Banner : 2340 × 1560 px (3:2, main item page header)
  Thumbnail      : 80 × 80 px    (icon shown in listings)
  Gallery images : 1200 × 800 px each (up to 15, feature screenshots)


──────────────────────────────────────────────
1. PREVIEW IMAGE — 590 × 300 px
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Create a horizontal preview banner for a premium Next.js portfolio
template called "payload-portfolio". Size: 590 × 300 px.

Layout (left-to-right):
  Left 55% — dark (zinc-950 #09090b) panel showing a minimal browser
  chrome mockup of a portfolio homepage. Inside the mockup: a dark
  sidebar rail with a small avatar circle and nav dots, and a hero
  section with a large bold name heading "Your Name" in white, a
  small indigo availability badge that reads "Available", and a
  subheadline in muted grey. Keep the mockup flat and clean — no
  drop shadows, pixel-perfect borders.

  Right 45% — solid indigo (#6366f1) panel with:
    - Top: white bold text "payload-portfolio" (18 px)
    - Middle: three short feature lines in white/80 opacity, each
      preceded by a small checkmark icon:
          ✓ Next.js 16 + Payload CMS 3
          ✓ No-code personalisation
          ✓ One-click Vercel deploy
    - Bottom: a small grey pill badge "v1.0.0"

Typography: sans-serif, clean and modern.
Overall feel: professional, dark-mode SaaS product.

to Inter or Geist) at ~48 px. The letterform should feel like a product

and a strong weight.

Simple, readable at small sizes.
